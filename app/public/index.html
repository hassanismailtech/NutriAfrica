How review works

Trust is a record of what was checked, not a green badge.

Photographs are evidence

We document what a label declares. Label transcription is not an independent chemical analysis. Two people reading the same photograph do not create two independent sources.

Separate checks

Source
An original packaged-food label, with capture date and market.
Review
A named reviewer checks the evidence before approving public facts.
Missing information
Unshown or unreadable information is recorded as unknown, never silently filled.
Registration
No regulator lookup is connected in this pilot. Printed numbers remain label observations.
Safety and allergens
No physical-pack authenticity, food-safety or allergen-free assurance is provided.

Start with labels, not guesses

Generic food composition, recipe estimates and measured portions are separate research tasks. This pilot does not infer meal calories from photographs or recommend treatment.

Collect safely

Photograph packaging you own or have permission to photograph. Respect store rules. Do not include faces, children, receipts, addresses or payment screens. Do not confront staff or act as a regulator.

Withdraw a contribution

Use the private receipt issued when you submitted.

Withdrawal removes the contribution and its stored images from this local service. A withdrawn entry will no longer be in the public catalogue. Backup and downstream-copy policy must be settled before public launch.

Terms & privacy draft

Pilot version: pilot-v1. Local testing only. Not approved for public collection.

What this test does

NutriAfrica stores a product description and the label photographs you submit. It collects no required name, email, phone number, health profile or precise GPS location. Server security controls may process network addresses transiently. Do not include personal information in photographs or label text.

Contribution permission

You confirm you are an adult and own the photographs or may supply them. For this test you grant nonexclusive permission to store, inspect, transcribe and sanitise them, and to publish approved label facts. Images are private and are not licensed for public redistribution in this version. Brand names and packaging artwork remain their owners’ property. This draft does not grant commercial resale rights for photographs or endorse a manufacturer.

Control and withdrawal

Keep your private receipt to withdraw a contribution. The withdrawal form removes the active record and images. No automated forwarding to Open Food Facts, regulator or AI service occurs. No marketing cookies or session replay are included.

Before a live release

The operating controller, privacy contact, hosting region, processor agreements, retention schedule, review owner and public contribution licence must be confirmed. Until then this service is a local preview for synthetic fixtures, not a public intake destination.

Limits

Labels may be incomplete, outdated or wrong. This pilot provides no nutritional, medical, regulatory or authenticity certification. Do not use it for allergy or treatment decisions.
